Six agents, one pipeline
Every score comes with a plain-language list of what drove it — the confidence number is never the whole answer.
01 · PREDICT

Defect risk scoring

Continuous 0–100% risk per machine, blending disclosed-weight rule thresholds with a per-sensor IsolationForest — not a single opaque score.

02 · DIAGNOSE

Root cause & routing

Splits a defect across equipment, material, process, and human factors, and routes it to whoever can actually act on it.

03 · RECOMMEND

Grounded corrective actions

Suggests a specific fix backed by precedent from resolved past cases in the knowledge base — never a guess dressed up as an answer.

04 · FORECAST

Pre-production risk

Scores a scheduled batch before it even runs, from its material lot, machine health, and shift history.

05 · TRACE

Exact unit traceability

Turns a foreign-object or scrap-debris event into a precise, quarantinable range of unit numbers — not "something may be wrong."

06 · ASK

A chatbot with one source of truth

Ask "why is line 3 red" and get an answer pulled from the same tables the dashboard reads — scoped to your role, every time.
